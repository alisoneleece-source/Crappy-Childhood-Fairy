Meet Anna Runkle

She teaches what she had to learn for herself.

Anna is best known as the Crappy Childhood Fairy. Her work grew from her own experience with the effects of early trauma and the practical techniques she developed and shared with others.

Her teaching now spans free tools, books, online courses, live programs and a large YouTube community.
