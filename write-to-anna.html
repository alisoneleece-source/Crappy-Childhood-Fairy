Write to Anna

Send Anna a focused 500–1,000 word letter with one clear question about a challenging situation in your life.

What Anna is looking for

Questions about challenging situations with friends, partners, dating, work, and family. Tell her what the problem is and what you’d like help with.

Before you write

What makes a strong letter?

  1. Write 500–1,000 words.Longer letters will not be accepted.
  2. Keep your letter focused on one question.Tell Anna what the problem is and what you would like help with.
  3. Keep background short.If background is needed, it should be no more than half of your letter.
  4. Choose a situation Anna can respond to.Questions can be about friends, partners, dating, work, or family.
  5. Skip explicit trauma details.Please do not include explicit descriptions of trauma.
  6. Protect other people’s privacy.Do not include names or specific identifying details about other people.

Anna’s team will let you know if your letter is selected.
