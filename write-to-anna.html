Write to Anna

Have a question you’d like Anna to answer? Keep it focused, protect your privacy, and tell her what you most want help understanding.

A quick note

Anna is not a doctor or therapist. Her answers and content are for informational purposes and are not a substitute for professional care.

Before you write

What makes a strong letter?

  1. Start with your question.Lead with the thing you want Anna to answer.
  2. Keep it concise.Anna currently asks for letters of about 1,000 words or less.
  3. Add only the context that helps.A little background is useful; a full history is usually not needed.
  4. Protect everyone’s privacy.Remove identifying details about yourself and other people.
  5. Skip graphic detail.Please do not include graphic descriptions of sex, violence, or abuse.
  6. Choose a broadly useful question.Topics that may help many viewers are more likely to be selected.

Only a small percentage of letters are chosen for Anna to answer publicly.
