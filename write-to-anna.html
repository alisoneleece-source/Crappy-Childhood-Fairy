Before you write
500–1,000 words.Longer letters can’t be accepted.
One question.Tell Anna what’s happening and what you’d like help with.
Keep background short.No more than half of your letter.
Skip explicit trauma details.Anna can help without them.
Protect others’ privacy.Leave out names and identifying details.
