Does this sound familiar?

Have you found that…

  • You have trouble focusing, or you procrastinate on things that matter
  • Your thoughts loop, replaying conversations and worrying about what’s next
  • You keep falling for people who aren’t available
  • You overreact, then feel ashamed and pull away
  • Making and keeping friends feels harder than it should
  • You’re tired all the time, or you swing between wired and shut down
It’s not your fault.

These are signs of dysregulation, the way a nervous system adapts to growing up with chaos, neglect or danger. It isn’t a character flaw. It’s neurological, and it can heal.
